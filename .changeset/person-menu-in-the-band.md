The signed-in person's own things are now in one menu under their name at
the right end of the band, at every width, a phone's included. It holds, in
this order, their settings, the resident preview where it is offered, and
signing out. The separate preview and sign-out buttons have left the band, the
phone's menu sheet no longer carries the preview, and the person's own
settings are no longer a section of the navigation. The board's settings for
the association stay in the board's section.

The person's own settings are renamed "Your settings" ("Dina inställningar"),
in the menu and as the page's heading, so they are not confused with the
association's settings. During a support session the menu is the supporter's,
under their name and role, and signing out from it ends the support session as
before.
