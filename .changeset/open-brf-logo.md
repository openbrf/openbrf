Show the Open BRF logo.

The client has a favicon now: the Open BRF mark, a building front with one
window lit in brass, which follows the browser's light or dark chrome, with an
ICO for browsers that take no SVG icon and an icon for a phone's home screen.

The screens outside the application frame - sign-in, activation, the account
request form, the setup wizard and the app consent screen - carry the logo above
their heading, in the version drawn for the page's light or dark theme. Inside
the frame nothing changes: the band still carries the association's identity.
