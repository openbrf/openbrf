The data subject access report now carries the internal notes on the issues a
person reported, and the issues somebody else reported that concern them. The
board weighs what of their text is given, as GDPR art. 15(4) asks.

- **The reporter's internal notes.** Each issue a person reported lists the
  internal notes on it by date, never who wrote them. The reporter never sees a
  note on their own screen, and a note may hold words about somebody else, so
  each one is given only as the board weighed it: whole, or left out because it
  is about somebody else.
- **Issues others reported that concern you.** A new section lists each issue a
  handler recorded as concerning the person, which somebody else reported. It
  always gives the issue's number, type, dates, where it stands, how it ended,
  how many photographs it holds and whom it was sent to. Its description,
  location, messages and internal notes are given only as the board weighed
  each: whole, or left out because it would tell who reported the issue or is
  about somebody else. It never names the reporter or any other source.
- **Left out, with a reason.** The report prints the reason for each text left
  out and none of its words. It also says that something was left out under
  art. 15(4) and that the person may complain to IMY.
- **The board weighs on the report's screen.** Whoever may produce the report
  opens each such issue from it, reads its texts and gives or leaves out each
  one. A text nobody has weighed yet is listed as not given. The screen counts
  those texts and will not print the report until each one is weighed. A note
  written after the board weighed an issue is not given until it is weighed.
- **Purged with the text.** A weighing goes with the text it weighs, with the
  record of whom the issue concerns, and with the issue's free text when the
  clock or a granted erasure request removes it. The audit log records the
  weighing against the person whose report it is, with how many texts were
  given and left out. It never names the issue or any of the text.
- **Wording.** The handlers' hint on internal notes, the intro to whom an issue
  concerns and the record of processing activities' issues row say so in
  Swedish and English.
