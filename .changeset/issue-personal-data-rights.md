The data subject access report and the report forms now cover what the issue
module holds about a person.

- **The reporter's access report** lists each issue they reported with its
  number, when it was closed, and the conversation on it as they read it on
  their own screen: their messages and the handlers', each handler's signed
  by the board or the property manager and never by a person. A removed
  message says when it was removed, and a conversation the purge has
  emptied says when it was purged. Each issue states the earliest day its
  free text, conversation and photographs may be purged, on the clock of its
  issue type, or that a legal hold on the issue keeps them.
- **Internal notes stay out of every access report**, the reporter's
  included, while the legal review decides whether GDPR art. 15 gives a
  reporter the notes the handlers keep about their issue.
- **The report forms say how long the report is kept.** The residents' form
  states the number of years after closing that the issue's free text is
  kept, and that a contractor is sent only what a handler writes for them.
  The website's form states that the reporter's name and email address are
  removed a year after the issue closes, and what they wrote and the
  replies they receive after the board's number of years.
- **The record of processing activities** names phone among the
  data the issue reports processing holds, for the contractor list, and says
  that a contractor stays on the list until the board removes them. The
  product's text reaches a board's record only where the board has not
  edited that row.
- **An erasure request about a name in an issue** now says that a handler
  carries it out by removing the text from the issue, and that the purge
  removes an issue's free text on its own clock after closing. It no longer
  says that the purge never rewrites a description.
