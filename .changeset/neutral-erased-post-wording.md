Word an erased message neutrally: it was erased, and the comments under it
follow their own retention rules. The feed's record of processing and the
glossary now say that a post or comment can also be erased early, when its
writer's request for erasure is granted, and that a legal hold or a restriction
can keep a comment past its year.
The notice above a group's message field says the same.
