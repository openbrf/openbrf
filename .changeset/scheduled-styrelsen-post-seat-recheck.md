A post the board scheduled as Styrelsen is published only if the board member
who scheduled it still speaks for the board when its time comes. If their seat
has ended meanwhile, the post stays a draft, is not mailed, and is not shown to
anybody outside the board. The board that remains still sees it among the
scheduled posts beside the feed and can withdraw it there, including a post to
Styrelsen or Administratörer, which is not on the news screen.
