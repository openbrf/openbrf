The sentence that a reply on an issue is not a notice to remedy now says how the
association sends one: outside Open BRF, for example by registered letter to the
tenant-owner's usual address, which counts as given even if the letter is not
collected (BRL 7 kap. 32 §), and that a notice sent in another way has to be
provable. The text a board reads about the property manager now says that the
association's processor agreement with them must also cover their work in
Open BRF.
