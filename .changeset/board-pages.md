The board's work now has pages of its own, under the board's section of the
navigation. Handling issues, managing bookings, handling motions, deciding
subletting applications, handling key orders, managing events, managing the
archive, reading every apartment's binder, reviewing reported posts and
arranging the general meeting each have a page there. The screens residents use
for issues, bookings, motions, subletting, keys, events, the archive, the
apartment binder and the feed no longer hold a board half, and a board member
sees the same screen there as their neighbours do. Editing a document on the
archive's shelf and striking through a comment or a post stay where they are,
under the board's sign, and so do the board's own posts waiting in the feed for
their time.

The board's section is grouped by the kind of work: queues, registers, what the
board publishes, finances and data protection. A plugin's view offered in the
board's section stands among the queues, after the core's own, rather than at
the end of the section. An account with two or more of the board's pages also
gets a front page, "To handle", with one row for each queue it works and what is
waiting in it. The overview no longer lists the queues: whoever works one gets a
single line above the house's panels saying how much is waiting, with a link to
the front page.

Every board page carries the board's sign on its heading and a sentence under it
saying who sees the page, so a page says whose it is on a phone as well. The
issue queue is signed "Board and property manager". An account a board page is
not offered to, opening its address, reads one sentence saying the page is the
board's, with no sign; that includes a resident opening the address book, which
now shows the board's register alone. The address book, the registers, the
import, the board mailbox, writing news, the website, fees, charges, data
protection and the general meeting have moved under `/board`; their old
addresses no longer exist. On a phone, an account with
board pages has the overview, its first board page and the feed in the bar. The
external property manager's one page, the issue queue, is their whole board
section and their bar's middle button. Nothing changes in who may do what.
