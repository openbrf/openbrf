Screens that residents and the board share now keep the board's work apart. On
bookings, issues, motions, subletting, keys, events, documents, the apartment
binder and chat, what a resident does comes first. Below it is one zone with
what only the board does there, signed "Board" and named by what it is for,
such as "Manage bookings". The issue queue is signed "Board and property
manager", because the property manager works it too. A resident sees neither
the zone nor its sign.

Board controls inside content everybody reads carry the same sign in a small
form: editing a document, the document filter, striking through a news
comment, and recording a board position. Granting a system role is signed
"Administrator", because only the administrator may do it. Nothing changes in
who may do what. Every zone and sign is shown from the same capability as the
function it marks.
