The settings now tell the board's sections from a member's own. "My account"
stays first and bare. The association's sections follow under a heading,
"Association administration", that carries the board's sign, and each of them
puts its panels in one board zone named by the section. A resident sees only
"My account" and no sign. Nothing changes in who may do what.
