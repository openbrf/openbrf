# Glossary

The canonical mapping between Swedish domain terms and the English terms used in code, APIs, and documentation. Swedish cooperative law is the spec for Open BRF, so precision here matters: **use these English terms and no others** for identifiers, i18n keys, and docs. When the legal concept is the point, mention the Swedish term in a doc comment.

Statutes referenced below:

- **BRL** - Bostadsrättslagen (1991:614), the Swedish Tenant-Ownership Act
- **EFL** - Lag om ekonomiska föreningar (2018:672), the Swedish Economic Associations Act

| Swedish | English (canonical) | Meaning / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| andelstal | participation share | The apartment's share of the association, used for fee allocation. |
| avisering | fee notification | Issuing payment notices (OCR numbers etc.). |
| boende | resident | Anyone living in the building: members, partners, tenants, children 13+. Not every resident is a member. |
| bokning | **booking** | One claim on one bookable resource for one period. Service tier: personal data held to run the calendar and for nothing else, so it is erased on its own clock a year after the booked period ends rather than on the retention policy that governs a former resident - a booking's purpose ends when the booking does, whether or not whoever made it still lives here. It is cancelled with a status rather than deleted, and who booked what is recorded in the audit log. The database holds one live booking per resource and start time, so a double booking is refused there and not by the application. |
| bokningsbar resurs | **bookable resource** | Something in the house that residents book: a laundry room (tvättstuga), the common room (föreningslokal), the guest apartment (gästlägenhet), a sauna (bastu). The board names its own rather than picking from a fixed list, so what a resource is is free text; what the platform knows is how it is booked - fixed-length slots inside a day, one whole day at a time, or a range of nights. It carries what one apartment may hold, concurrently and per calendar week, each limit optional and neither implying the other. Withdrawn from booking rather than deleted, because the bookings made against it say what they were for only through it. Holds no personal data, so it is outside every purge scope. |
| bostadsrätt | tenant-ownership; cooperative apartment | The ownership form: a share in the association tied to the right to use an apartment. |
| bostadsrättsförening (BRF) | housing cooperative; keep **BRF** as abbreviation | The association itself. "BRF" is established and used as-is in names and prose. |
| bostadsrättsregister | cooperative housing register | The state register of cooperative apartments kept by Lantmäteriet (Lag (2026:484) om bostadsrättsregister, 3 kap.). The cooperative reports grant, transfer and termination, each within two weeks, though the transfer window runs from the membership decision and the statute assigns the report to a juridical person in defined cases; liens are reported by the lienholder, not the cooperative. |
| dataskyddsförordningen | GDPR | Keep "GDPR". |
| debitering | **charge** | A one-off cost put on a named member or apartment: a key to the bike room, a replacement tag, a subletting fee, a repair charged on. Open BRF records the basis for the charge - amount, date, reason, VAT treatment - and never the payment; the ledger belongs to the accounting system. |
| debiteringslängd | **debiting list** | In Open BRF: the export of charge basis per member or apartment, handed to whoever keeps the association's books as CSV or PDF. The statutory debiteringslängd of a joint facility association (samfällighetsförening) is a separate artifact governed by Lag (1973:1150) om förvaltning av samfälligheter, 42 §, which requires it to state the amount assessed, what falls on each member and when payment is due. |
| dokumentarkiv | **document archive** | The association's own documents - bylaws (stadgar), minutes (protokoll), house rules (trivselregler), the annual report (årsredovisning) - each carrying the audience it is for: the board, the members, or anyone. Association records rather than personal data, so outside the purge scope. Minutes of a general meeting name the members who spoke, so they are the members' unless the board publishes a particular set deliberately. |
| dokumentlista | **document list** | A block on a page of the association's website that lists the document archive. It carries no documents and no audience: what it lists is resolved against whoever is reading, by the archive's own rule, so a page anyone can open shows the public shelf to a visitor and the members' shelf as well to a member. The board's own shelf is on no page - every serve of one of those files is audited and gated by a capability, and the website has neither. At most it names one binder (pärm) to narrow to. |
| ekonomisk förvaltare | **economic manager** | The external bookkeeping and fee-invoicing service many associations buy (SBC, Nabo, Fastum and the like). Distinct from *property manager* (förvaltare/vicevärd), who handles the building and issues; an economic manager receives exports and has no account in Open BRF. |
| fastighetsbeteckning | property designation | The property's identifier with Lantmäteriet, e.g. "Talgoxen 4". Recorded by the board as an association fact and published on the broker information page; it names the property, never an apartment. |
| felanmälan | **issue report** | Resident-reported maintenance issue; the module is "issues". |
| föreningsfakta | **association facts** | What the board records about the association itself - the property designation, the year built, whether the land is owned or held on a site leasehold, how the fee is set and what it covers, the transfer and pledge fee policies, parking, storage, renovations. Recorded once on the board's own screen and published in two places by one renderer: the broker information page (mäklarinfo), and an association facts block on a page the board arranges. A question nobody has answered is on neither. |
| föreningsstämma / stämma | **general meeting** | Annual or extraordinary meeting of members. Postal voting is prohibited in a BRF (BRL 9 kap. 14 §) and is never built. |
| förtroendeuppdrag | **position of trust** | An elected seat on the board and the period it ran for: chair (ordförande), board member (styrelseledamot), deputy (suppleant). Elected by the general meeting rather than appointed, which is why recording one is the board's own act and never an administrator's, and why the election carries the date the meeting was held rather than the day it was typed in. A dated period and not a flag: ending a term writes the date it ended and leaves the period on file, because who answered for the association between two dates is what the record is for. Its end date may be in the future - a term recorded in April as running to the annual meeting still confers what a seat confers until the date arrives - so it is bounded to a plausible horizon and stays correctable until it passes. The model, the payload field and the capability are named board position (`BoardPosition`, `boardPositions`, `boardPosition:manage`); "board seat" is the same thing said of one row. Not a system role: those are held today or not, and are the administrator's to grant. |
| förvaltare / vicevärd | **property manager** | External role with access to issue handling only, never the address book. |
| gallring | purging | GDPR retention deletion in the service tier; the statutory archive tier is exempt (7-year lock). |
| granskningslogg | **audit log** | The append-only record of accesses and changes (AuditLogEntry). Never "access log": the record covers changes as well as reads, and the retention screen has to say which record a retention policy cannot reach. |
| inflyttning / utflyttning | **move-in** / **move-out** | The flow that starts or ends a residency. A move-in as a member writes the member register entry; a move-out writes the exit entry when the person's last tenant-ownership ends. |
| insats | initial share capital | The original capital contribution tied to an apartment. |
| integritetspolicy | **privacy notice** | The association's own account of how it processes personal data, which GDPR art. 12-14 require it to provide. Shipped as a fill-in page - the headings a notice has to answer and no text under them - because what a cooperative does with personal data differs between cooperatives, and a canned policy that is wrong about a particular one reads as a statement the board never made. Published and linked from every page's footer from the start, so the obligation is reachable while the board is still writing it. |
| kontaktformulär | **contact form** | The form on the association's public website for writing to the board without an account. A message is stored first and the board is notified by email afterwards, so a mail server that is down cannot lose it; the inbox in settings is the record. Distinct from the felanmälan (issue report), which produces an ärende rather than correspondence. |
| lägenhetsförteckning | **apartment register** | Statutory register of apartments incl. liens and share capital (BRL 9 kap.). Confidential - only the tenant-owner may see their own entry. Never blended with the member register. |
| lägenhetsnummer | apartment number | Lantmäteriet numbering (e.g. 1101). Always rendered in the mono grid. |
| Lantmäteriet | Lantmäteriet | Swedish mapping/cadastral authority; register export deadline Dec 2027. Keep the name. |
| mäklarinfo | broker information | The public page brokers and buyers expect: fees, transfer and lien note fees, building facts, links to bylaws and annual reports. Generated from association facts - never from the statutory registers. The transactional broker extract (mäklarbild) is a paid module, not core. |
| medlem | member | A person who is a member of the association. |
| medlemsförteckning | **member register** | Statutory register of members. BRL 9 kap. 8 § requires it and 9 § fixes its contents and its availability to anyone who asks; EFL 5 kap. 6-7 §§ apply through BRL 9 kap. 9 b §. Must never be blended with the apartment register in UI or data model. |
| meny | **menu** | The navigation on the association's website: a top level and one level under it, built from the association's own pages, the pages the platform generates and addresses elsewhere. The menu is also the ordering of the site - its first page entry is the front page, so there is no separate home-page setting. A visitor is offered only the entries they could open, so a page kept for the members is not named to anyone without a session. |
| motion | motion | Member proposal to the general meeting. |
| nyhet / nyheter | **news item** / **news** | What the board writes to the people who live in the house and to whoever reads the association's website. A news item is for the members unless the board publishes it to anyone, and it is served under /nyheter. It is the association's own writing rather than register data: monolingual, stored as written, and never translated. |
| nyhetsutskick | **news mailing** | The email that goes to the members when a news item is published, in each recipient's own language. The email specifically, not the act of reaching them: the text message is a smsutskick, claimed separately. Claimed once per item and never sent again - correcting a published notice mails nobody - and who it went to is written down as it stood at the moment of publication. |
| ordförande | chair | Board chair - a position of trust (brass in the design system). |
| pantsättning / pantnotering | **lien** / lien note | Recorded in the apartment register; confidential. |
| pantsättningsavgift | pledge fee | What the association charges for noting a lien. Permitted by BRL 7 kap. 14 § only where the bylaws provide for it; that section sets no ceiling on it. An association fact - the policy and who pays it - and never a per-apartment amount. A pending version of that section, in force on a day the government sets, drops the pledge fee from the fees an association may charge at all, leaving the grant, transfer and second-hand letting fees; it follows the lien moving to the state register, which the lienholder reports rather than the association. |
| pass | **slot** | One bookable period of a resource booked in time slots: a laundry room open 07:00 to 21:00 in two-hour slots offers seven a day. Fixed-length and derived from the resource's slot length and opening hours rather than stored, so narrowing the hours changes which slots exist without touching any booking; the length is carried on the resource as `slotMinutes`. What a booking claims, and the unit the database's one-live-booking-per-resource-and-start-time rule is written against. A slot that has begun reads as passed rather than as free, because a calendar of the week a resident is looking at has days in it that have gone. |
| personnummer | personal identity number | Swedish national ID. Only where legally justified; never in public views. |
| publiceringssamtycke | **publication consent** | A person's recorded consent to appear on a page the association publishes. No personal data reaches a public page without one, and protected personal data never reaches one at all. An image upload therefore declares whether it shows identifiable persons: that declaration is what ties a photograph to this consent. One consent covers one scope - a photograph, a name on the website, or the published board roster - and agreeing to one is not agreeing to another. It is granted and withdrawn as dated facts: a withdrawal closes the consent with a date and never deletes the record that it stood, because the period it covered is what says a page published then was published lawfully. The picture scope is named for that scope in code - the confirmation `photoConsentConfirmed` and the refusal `photo-consent-required` - and never for the consent as a whole; what the board reads on the screen says publiceringssamtycke. |
| pärm | **binder** | What a document in the archive is filed into - Protokoll, Stadgar, Trivselregler, Årsredovisning. The board names its own rather than picking from a fixed list, so it is free text the form only suggests from. Carried on the document as `category`, the archive's word for what a binder holds; the term for the binder itself is "binder", never "folder". |
| registerutdrag | data subject access report | Per-person GDPR report. |
| rättsligt bevarandekrav | **legal hold** | A recorded reason for keeping one person's service-tier data past its purge date: a dispute, an insurance matter, a request from an authority. GDPR art. 17.3 e (a legal claim to establish or defend) and art. 17.3 b (a legal obligation to keep the data) are the grounds it rests on: they disapply the right to erasure rather than supplying a basis for processing, which the association still needs under art. 6. It suspends purging for that person alone rather than changing the association's retention policy, and it is released with a date rather than deleted, so the record explains why nothing was purged in that period. |
| samtycke | **consent** | A recorded agreement that is the legal basis for processing personal data (GDPR art. 6.1 a). The board's samtycke to a plugin's stated permissions and personal-data categories is what lets it run, and a republished version that widens either is refused until the board consents again. The per-person case of appearing on a published page is publiceringssamtycke, below. |
| sida | **page** | One page on the association's own website, at its own address directly under the domain root. Written by the board, published or held as a draft, and either public or readable by anyone signed in - a page nobody may read is answered exactly as one that does not exist. Its body is a list of blocks and never markup, so the renderer decides what a block becomes and no stored page can carry a script or make the reader's browser fetch anything from a third party - a picture names a stored file by its id and never a URL. A link the board writes is the one address on a page that may leave this origin, and it is a navigation the visitor chooses rather than something fetched while the page is read. Service tier: the cooperative's own writing about itself, not register content. |
| skyddade personuppgifter | **protected personal data** | Persons with protected identity: masked in all public views, exports, and lists; access is logged. |
| smsadapter | **SMS adapter** | The interface an SMS provider is written against, and the only way out of the platform by text message. One interface and a driver behind it, like the file storage: no provider's package is a dependency of the application, so a cooperative changes provider by changing a setting rather than by waiting for a release. An instance with no driver configured has no provider, which is where every instance starts. |
| smsutskick | **SMS mailing** | The text message that goes to the members when a news item is published: a headline and the address of the article, in each recipient's own language. Not a nyhetsutskick, which is the email - the two are asked for separately, claimed separately and reported separately, and a member reached both ways has been reached once each way. It goes to the members the association holds a number for; a member without one is not among the people it can reach this way, which is an absence rather than a failed delivery. Claimed once per item and never sent again. |
| stadgar | bylaws | The association's statutes. |
| styrelse | **board** | The elected board of the association. |
| styrelseledamot / ledamot | board member | |
| styrelselista | **board roster** | Who the association's board is, as the association publishes it: a name and an elected position, and nothing else. Each name requires that person's own publication consent (publiceringssamtycke) for this scope, which is not the same as consenting to a photograph, and a person carrying protected personal data (skyddade personuppgifter) is never published whatever they have consented to. Read from the register when the page is rendered, so a consent withdrawn today is off the page today. |
| suppleant | deputy board member | |
| systemroll | **system role** | A grant held against the instance rather than against an apartment or an election: administrator, and the external property manager. Separate from a position of trust, which is elected and dated, and from a residency, which follows the apartment. Held or not held rather than kept as history, and conferred from the person's own panel by an administrator. |
| tema | **theme** | An installable package that restyles an instance. The other package type beside a plugin, and not a kind of one: a theme is pure data - a manifest, light and dark token sets, fonts, a logo and a choice of core view variants - and contains no JavaScript, so it declares no permissions, handles no personal data, and puts nothing in the running process. It decides what a token looks like and never what it means, because colour carries legal semantics in a statutory register. Checked against a contrast matrix at install time, and installed or switched without a restart, since there is no code to load. |
| tillägg | **plugin** | An installable package that adds behaviour or a view to an instance. Installed from the curated catalog, declares what it may do and which personal data it handles, and is consented to by the board before it runs. Its code runs in the instance's own process, so installing or switching one on ends by restarting it. A theme is the other package type and is not a plugin. |
| tomträtt | site leasehold | The association holding its land on a leasehold from a public owner - the state, a municipality or other land in public ownership - rather than owning it. Governed by Jordabalken 13 kap. Recorded as an association fact, with the ground rent and the renegotiation date, because it is the first thing a broker asks about the land. |
| upphörande | termination | A bostadsrätt ceasing to exist. A register event in its own right, distinct from a transfer to a new holder; reported by the cooperative (Lag (2026:484), 3 kap.). |
| upplåtelse | grant | The act of first granting a bostadsrätt to a holder; reported by the cooperative (Lag (2026:484), 3 kap.). The contract that does so is the upplåtelseavtal (grant agreement). |
| upplåtelseavtal | grant agreement | The contract first granting a bostadsrätt. |
| utdrag ur förteckning | **register extract** | A copy of a statutory register, produced on request. The member register extract is public; the apartment register extract goes to the board and to the tenant-owner it concerns. Distinct from the GDPR registerutdrag, which is a data subject access report. |
| utflyttad | moved out | Register state with dashed outline + purge date in the UI. |
| utskickskanal | **delivery channel** | Which way one recipient's copy of a mailing was sent: email or SMS. Carried on each row of the delivery ledger, so the two channels share one record of who the board addressed while each keeps its own claim, its own worker and its own report. |
| vanliga frågor | **FAQ** | The questions a housing cooperative is asked and the board's answers, carried in the block on the page rather than in a store of their own: it is the association's own writing about its own house, and the page it sits on is the thing that is published. Monolingual and stored as written, like every other piece of site content. An answer is one paragraph; an answer that needs several is a page, linked from here. |
| vistelse | **stay** | What a booking of a resource booked by the night is to whoever makes it: a check-in, the nights between, and a check-out. Not a model of its own - a stay is one booking against a resource in the date-range mode, and its end is the local midnight after the last night, which is exactly the check-out date - so the word belongs to the interface and to the form that collects the two dates, and never to a table, a column or a payload field. Unbroken by definition: a night somebody else holds ends a stay rather than being passed over, which is why a range covering one is refused whole. |
| årsavgift / månadsavgift | annual fee / monthly fee | The fee the tenant-owner pays the association. |
| ärende | **issue** | One reported problem with the building and the record of handling it, from new through in progress to done. The felanmälan (issue report) is how it arrives; the ärende is what the board and the property manager work. Service tier, and among the most sensitive free text the platform holds - a report about a leak or a neighbour carries health data and a third party's details without anybody intending it - so it is readable by its reporter and by whoever handles issues, and never published. |
| ärendetyp | **issue type** | The category an issue is reported under. Configured by the board, monolingual, and scoped to exactly one audience: non-member, member, or board (internal). The audience decides who is offered the type, and the filter is applied on the server for every caller - a resident is never shown the internal categories, and the form on the public website offers the non-member ones alone. |
| överlåtelse | transfer | Sale/transfer of a bostadsrätt between holders. |
| överlåtelseavgift | transfer fee | What the association charges when a bostadsrätt changes hands. Permitted by BRL 7 kap. 14 § only where the bylaws provide for it; that section sets no ceiling on it. An association fact - the policy and who pays it - and never a per-transfer amount. |
| överlåtelseavtal | **transfer agreement** | The contract transferring a bostadsrätt, as the upplåtelseavtal (grant agreement) is the one that first grants it. |
| överlåtelseavtalets referens | **agreement reference** | What the apartment register records to identify a transfer's transfer agreement: the board's own reference - a case number, or where the paper copy is filed - or the path of an uploaded copy. Every transfer states one, because the register extract lists it and a transfer cannot be removed once written. Never "contract number": the reference need not be a number and need not come from the contract. |

## Adding terms

Add the term in the same PR that introduces it in code, keep the table alphabetized by the Swedish column, and cite the statute at chapter level (only cite an exact section when verified against the current text at [riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se)).

Alphabetized in the Swedish alphabet, where å, ä and ö are letters in their own right and sort after z rather than as variants of a and o. `årsavgift` therefore follows `utflyttad`, and the `överlåtelse` rows come last.
