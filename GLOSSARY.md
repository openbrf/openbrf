# Glossary

The canonical mapping between Swedish domain terms and the English terms used in code, APIs, and documentation. Swedish cooperative law is the spec for Open BRF, so precision here matters: **use these English terms and no others** for identifiers, i18n keys, and docs. When the legal concept is the point, mention the Swedish term in a doc comment.

Statutes referenced below:

- **BRL** - Bostadsrättslagen (1991:614), the Swedish Tenant-Ownership Act
- **EFL** - Lag om ekonomiska föreningar (2018:672), the Swedish Economic Associations Act
- **Lag (2026:484)** om bostadsrättsregister, with its companion Lag (2026:485) om
  införande av den lagen. Both are enacted; 2026:484 enters into force on a day the
  government sets, 2026:485 on 1 January 2027. Lag (2026:486) is the amending act that
  writes the registration duties into BRL, and those sections appear in the
  consolidated text under a *Träder i kraft* heading beside the version still in force.

| Swedish | English (canonical) | Meaning / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| andelstal | participation share | The apartment's share of the association, used for fee allocation. |
| anmälan | **sign-up** | A person putting their name down for one occurrence (tillfälle) of an event: the cleaning day on the 18th of April, not the series. Offered or not on the event as a whole, and counted against one occurrence, so twenty places at each of a year's cleaning days is twenty places twelve times over rather than twenty for the year. Withdrawn with a date rather than deleted, so who was expected and who stood down are both answerable afterwards. The personal data the event calendar holds - the event itself records only who entered it, the way a news item records its author - so it carries its own retention window and its own section in the data subject access report. Who has signed up is for whoever manages events; the public website carries a count at most, never a name. |
| anmälningsskyldighet | **reporting obligation** | One dated duty to report a register event to the cooperative housing register (bostadsrättsregistret), and the ledger of them the association keeps. Lag (2026:484) 3 kap. gives two weeks for each, counted from a day the section in question names rather than from one shared day: an övergång from the day the association decided on membership (3 kap. 3 § andra stycket), a termination from the day the bostadsrätt ceased (3 kap. 4 §), and an upplåtelse from the upplåtelse itself (3 kap. 2 §) - and 3 kap. 10 § lets Lantmäteriet order a late report in under penalty of a fine. Recorded in the same transaction as the register write it is computed from, never by a job that looks for events afterwards, because a ledger assembled later is one that can be missing a deadline nobody notices is absent. Statutory tier and strictly append-only, on the termination's reading: the event it reports cannot change and neither can the day the statute counts from, so discharging the duty is a separate later fact rather than an edit to the row. A lien opens none of these - 3 kap. 5 § puts that anmälan on the panthavare, who signs it with the pledgor - and the model is named `RegisterReportObligation` because which register it reports to is what distinguishes it from every other duty a board has a date for. |
| avanmälan | **withdrawal** | Standing down from a sign-up (anmälan) to one occurrence (tillfälle) of an event. A date written on the sign-up rather than a deleted row, so who was expected and who changed their mind are two different answers instead of one absence. The place is free the moment the date is written, because the places taken are the sign-ups without one - and it is takeable again by anybody, including by whoever stood down, at the back of the queue rather than in reserve. The person themselves does it, and whoever manages events may do it on their behalf: that is what lets a board deal with the sign-ups on a date it needs to move. A withdrawn sign-up never blocks the board from reshaping or removing the event, and it is kept and purged on exactly the same clock as a standing one, because both are records about the same date. |
| avisering | fee notification | Issuing payment notices (OCR numbers etc.). |
| beslut om medlemskap | **membership decision** | The board's decision to admit the acquirer of a bostadsrätt as a member, and the day it was taken. Recorded on the transfer as `membershipDecidedOn` because that day, and not the transfer, is when the cooperative housing register's two-week reporting window opens: Lag (2026:484) 3 kap. 3 § andra stycket runs the deadline "inom två veckor från det att bostadsrättsföreningen beslutat om medlemskap i föreningen". Nullable, and not a gap when absent: the same paragraph runs the window from the transfer itself where the acquirer is already a member or falls outside the membership requirement, and the section's fjärde stycket does the same where the bostadsrätt passed to the association ("Om en bostadsrätt har övergått till föreningen, ska anmälan göras inom två veckor från övergången"), so none of those transfers has a decision to date. Minuted by the board and derivable from nothing else the platform holds, which is why it is recorded when the decision is taken rather than reconstructed later. |
| biträde | **assistant** | Somebody a member or an ombud brings with them to the general meeting. EFL 6 kap. 7 § allows at most one each and gives it the right to speak at the meeting; that is the whole of what it grants, so an assistant carries no vote and is on the voting register (röstlängd) as a line without one - EFL 6 kap. 27 § lists the biträden present alongside the members and the ombud. Who may be one is the member's spouse or cohabitant or another member unless the bylaws (stadgar) say otherwise (BRL 9 kap. 14 §), and that clause is recorded as a setting and stated rather than enforced: a biträde needs no written authority at all, so there is no document for the board to attest and nothing for a check to key itself to. Recorded as attendance in a stated capacity, with the member or ombud who brought them named on the line.
| boende | resident | Anyone living in the building: members, partners, tenants, children 13+. Not every resident is a member. |
| bokning | **booking** | One claim on one bookable resource for one period. Service tier: personal data held to run the calendar and for nothing else, so it is erased on its own clock a year after the booked period ends rather than on the retention policy that governs a former resident - a booking's purpose ends when the booking does, whether or not whoever made it still lives here. It is cancelled with a status rather than deleted, and who booked what is recorded in the audit log. The database holds one live booking per resource and start time, so a double booking is refused there and not by the application. |
| bokningsbar resurs | **bookable resource** | Something in the house that residents book: a laundry room (tvättstuga), the common room (föreningslokal), the guest apartment (gästlägenhet), a sauna (bastu). The board names its own rather than picking from a fixed list, so what a resource is is free text; what the platform knows is how it is booked - fixed-length slots inside a day, one whole day at a time, or a range of nights. It carries what one apartment may hold, concurrently and per calendar week, each limit optional and neither implying the other. Withdrawn from booking rather than deleted, because the bookings made against it say what they were for only through it. Holds no personal data, so it is outside every purge scope. |
| bostadsrätt | tenant-ownership; cooperative apartment | The ownership form: a share in the association tied to the right to use an apartment. |
| bostadsrättsförening (BRF) | housing cooperative; keep **BRF** as abbreviation | The association itself. "BRF" is established and used as-is in names and prose. |
| bostadsrättsregister | cooperative housing register | The state register of cooperative apartments kept by Lantmäteriet (Lag (2026:484) om bostadsrättsregister, 3 kap.). The cooperative reports grant, transfer and termination, each within two weeks, though the transfer window runs from the membership decision and the statute assigns the report to a juridical person in defined cases; liens are reported by the lienholder, not the cooperative. |
| dagordning | **agenda** | The matters a general meeting (föreningsstämma) is to deal with, in the order it deals with them. EFL 6 kap. 22 § requires the notice (kallelse) to state them clearly, so the agenda is a running order rather than a set: each item carries its position, and two items cannot claim the same one. The board's own writing, stored as written and never translated. Each item may carry what the meeting decided about it, as the counts and the outcome the chair read out; an item the meeting deferred to a continued meeting carries no decision rather than a third outcome. Replaced whole rather than edited item by item - moving an item is the same act as adding one - and not replaceable once the meeting has been recorded as held, because from then on the agenda is the record of what the meeting dealt with.
| dataskyddsförordningen | GDPR | Keep "GDPR". |
| debitering | **charge** | A one-off cost put on a named member or apartment: a key to the bike room, a replacement tag, a subletting fee, a repair charged on. Open BRF records the basis for the charge - amount, date, reason, VAT treatment - and never the payment; the ledger belongs to the accounting system. |
| debiteringslängd | **debiting list** | In Open BRF: the export of charge basis per member or apartment, handed to whoever keeps the association's books as CSV or PDF. The statutory debiteringslängd of a joint facility association (samfällighetsförening) is a separate artifact governed by Lag (1973:1150) om förvaltning av samfälligheter, 42 §, which requires it to state the amount assessed, what falls on each member and when payment is due. |
| deltagarlista | **roll-call** | Who has put their name down for one occurrence (tillfälle) of an event, as whoever manages events reads it: the names, when each signed up, and the date any of them stood down (avanmälan). Personal data about the residents on it, so it is behind the managing capability alone - the calendar a resident reads carries how many places are gone and never who has them, and the public website carries a count at most. A person with protected personal data (skyddade personuppgifter) is on it as a place and never as a name: the statutory registers have a reason to print those names and a list read in a stairwell doorway has none. Somebody the register no longer holds is on it as a place too, because a sign-up outlives the person record a purge has reached. |
| dokumentarkiv | **document archive** | The association's own documents - bylaws (stadgar), minutes (protokoll), house rules (trivselregler), the annual report (årsredovisning) - each carrying the audience it is for: the board, the members, or anyone. Association records rather than personal data, so outside the purge scope. Minutes of a general meeting name the members who spoke, so they are the members' unless the board publishes a particular set deliberately. |
| dokumentlista | **document list** | A block on a page of the association's website that lists the document archive. It carries no documents and no audience: what it lists is resolved against whoever is reading, by the archive's own rule, so a page anyone can open shows the public shelf to a visitor and the members' shelf as well to a member. The board's own shelf is on no page - every serve of one of those files is audited and gated by a capability, and the website has neither. At most it names one binder (pärm) to narrow to. |
| ekonomisk förvaltare | **economic manager** | The external bookkeeping and fee-invoicing service many associations buy (SBC, Nabo, Fastum and the like). Distinct from *property manager* (förvaltare/vicevärd), who handles the building and issues; an economic manager receives exports and has no account in Open BRF. |
| evenemang | **event** | Something the association arranges and the series of dates it happens on: a cleaning day (städdag), a board meeting, a sauna evening, the general meeting. One event describes the whole series - what it is called, what it is about, the category the board sorts it under, where it happens, whether people may sign up (anmälan) and how many places each date has - and the dates are occurrences (tillfälle) of it. An event with no recurrence is an event with one occurrence rather than a second kind of thing. For the members unless the board publishes it to anyone, like a news item, and monolingual: the association's own content, stored as written and never translated. |
| fastighetsbeteckning | property designation | The property's identifier with Lantmäteriet, e.g. "Talgoxen 4". It names the property, never an apartment. Held twice, on purpose. The association's own authoritative record of it sits on the association singleton beside the organisation number, is recorded on the apartment register screen behind that register's capability, and is written to the audit log with what it replaced - it is register content, because the cooperative housing register holds data about the bostadsrättslägenhet (Lag (2026:484) 2 kap. 1 § första stycket 1) which the association has to supply (Lag (2026:485) 3 §), and 6 § of that act drops the duty where the data can be taken from fastighetsregistret or lägenhetsregistret instead - registers keyed on this designation. The field list is issued, and it makes this field conditional rather than general: Förordning (2026:898) 2 kap. 4 § första stycket registers the association's lagfarts- och tomträttsinnehav, and andra stycket - the last of that section's two paragraphs - replaces it with fastighetsbeteckning, taxeringsenhetsnummer and fastighetstyp where the association's buildings stand on land it neither owns nor holds with tomträtt. Whether that is so is answerable from the association facts, which already record whether the land is owned or held on a site leasehold. The section is in force from 1 January 2027 and the whole of it falls inside the initial supply duty (that förordning's övergångsbestämmelse 2). Taxeringsenhetsnummer and fastighetstyp have no field here and belong with the reporting work. Separately, the board writes one as an association fact for the broker information page: that one is published prose, and no statutory answer is derived from it. Corrected in place rather than dated, because a fastighetsbildning renames a property. |
| fastighetstyp | property type | One of the three fields Förordning (2026:898) 2 kap. 4 § andra stycket has an association report in place of its lagfarts- och tomträttsinnehav, in the conditional case the property designation row describes. That förordning names the field and does not define it, so what the value is drawn from is a question the reporting work has to answer rather than one this page can. The nearest statutory classification is the type of taxeringsenhet under fastighetstaxeringslagen (1979:1152) 4 kap. 5 §, which names småhusenhet, ägarlägenhetsenhet, hyreshusenhet, industrienhet, specialenhet, lantbruksenhet and elproduktionsenhet - and that classifies the taxeringsenhet, which is not always the property: a property is one unless something else is prescribed (4 kap. 1 §), while parts of one property in different ownership are split into several (2 §) and properties of one owner in one municipality are brought together into one (3 §). So the field is not safely read as an attribute of the designation beside it. Not recorded: the field is needed to render a report to the cooperative housing register, nothing in Open BRF renders one yet, and a column no code reads is a value nobody maintains. Listed here because the documentation names it, and named in the roadmap as a gap the reporting work closes. |
| felanmälan | **issue report** | Resident-reported maintenance issue; the module is "issues". |
| fullmakt | **proxy authorisation** | A member's written authority for somebody else to exercise their right at one general meeting. EFL 6 kap. 4 § requires it to be written, dated and signed by the member, and holds it good for at most one year from the day it was issued - which is why the day the member signed is recorded and measured against the meeting day, and measured again when the voting register (röstlängd) is drawn rather than trusted from the registration. A member may not be represented by more than one ombud. Open BRF records that the board saw the paper and never a signature of its own: a document that has to be signed under that Act may be signed with an advanced electronic signature (EFL 1 kap. 15 §), which is a trust service the free core does not provide. Taken back with a date rather than deleted, because a member who withdrew their authority has done something.
| föreningsfakta | **association facts** | What the board records about the association itself - the property designation, the year built, whether the land is owned or held on a site leasehold, how the fee is set and what it covers, the transfer and pledge fee policies, parking, storage, renovations. Recorded once on the board's own screen and published in two places by one renderer: the broker information page (mäklarinfo), and an association facts block on a page the board arranges. A question nobody has answered is on neither. |
| föreningsstämma / stämma | **general meeting** | Where the members exercise their right to decide the association's affairs (EFL 6 kap. 1 §). Ordinary (ordinarie) or extraordinary (extra); the meeting at which the annual report is laid is the ordinary one the statute puts those items on rather than a third kind, and the bylaws (stadgar) may require further ordinary meetings in a year. It carries the day it is held, which is the day that decides who has a vote, its agenda (dagordning), who was present and in what capacity, the written authorities (fullmakt) somebody else's vote was exercised under, and each item's outcome as the counts the chair read out. Arranged and then recorded as held: before, the agenda is a plan and the voting register (röstlängd) is a projection of the member register; afterwards, the agenda is what the meeting dealt with. Open BRF records the decisions and never the document - the minutes (protokoll) are filed in the document archive as association records, which is where EFL 6 kap. 39-40 §§ leave them. Postal voting is prohibited in a BRF and the meeting's powers may not be delegated to fullmäktige (BRL 9 kap. 14 §); neither is built. |
| förtroendeuppdrag | **position of trust** | An elected seat on the board and the period it ran for: chair (ordförande), board member (styrelseledamot), deputy (suppleant). Elected by the general meeting rather than appointed, which is why recording one is the board's own act and never an administrator's, and why the election carries the date the meeting was held rather than the day it was typed in. A dated period and not a flag: ending a term writes the date it ended and leaves the period on file, because who answered for the association between two dates is what the record is for. Its end date may be in the future - a term recorded in April as running to the annual meeting still confers what a seat confers until the date arrives - so it is bounded to a plausible horizon and stays correctable until it passes. The model, the payload field and the capability are named board position (`BoardPosition`, `boardPositions`, `boardPosition:manage`); "board seat" is the same thing said of one row. Not a system role: those are held today or not, and are the administrator's to grant. |
| förvaltare / vicevärd | **property manager** | External role with access to issue handling only, never the address book. |
| gallring | purging | GDPR retention deletion in the service tier; the statutory archive tier is exempt (7-year lock). |
| granskningslogg | **audit log** | The append-only record of accesses and changes (AuditLogEntry). Never "access log": the record covers changes as well as reads, and the retention screen has to say which record a retention policy cannot reach. |
| inflyttning / utflyttning | **move-in** / **move-out** | The flow that starts or ends a residency. A move-in as a member writes the member register entry; a move-out writes the exit entry when the person's last tenant-ownership ends. |
| insats | initial share capital | The original capital contribution tied to an apartment. |
| inställt tillfälle | **call-off** | Taking one date of an event off without touching the rest of its series. A date written on the occurrence (tillfälle) rather than a deleted row: it was announced, people may have signed up to it, and "the cleaning day on the 18th was called off" is a different thing to say than "there was never one". It withdraws nobody. Whoever had signed up (anmälan) still holds their place and is still counted against the capacity, because standing down is theirs to decide - and they may do it on a date that is off, which is what frees the place. No new sign-up is taken while it is off, and an edit that would move the date is refused exactly as it is for a date going ahead. Reversed by a reinstatement (återupptaget tillfälle), which is a second recorded act rather than this one undone. |
| integritetspolicy | **privacy notice** | The association's own account of how it processes personal data, which GDPR art. 12-14 require it to provide. Shipped as a fill-in page - the headings a notice has to answer and no text under them - because what a cooperative does with personal data differs between cooperatives, and a canned policy that is wrong about a particular one reads as a statement the board never made. Published and linked from every page's footer from the start, so the obligation is reachable while the board is still writing it. |
| kommentar | **comment** | One resident's reply under one news item, written inside the application. Exactly as visible as the news item it sits on and never rendered on the public website, whose pages read no session at all: a comment on a public notice is still not public. Service tier - personal data held so the people who live in the house can answer a notice - so it is erased on its own clock a year after it was written rather than on the retention policy that governs a former resident. A comment thread is the whole of what Open BRF offers here: a full discussion forum is deliberately never built and is integrated instead. |
| kontaktformulär | **contact form** | The form on the association's public website for writing to the board without an account. A message is stored first and the board is notified by email afterwards, so a mail server that is down cannot lose it; the inbox in settings is the record. Distinct from the felanmälan (issue report), which produces an ärende rather than correspondence. |
| Lantmäteriet | Lantmäteriet | Swedish mapping/cadastral authority; register export deadline Dec 2027. Keep the name. |
| lägenhetsförteckning | **apartment register** | Statutory register of apartments incl. liens and share capital (BRL 9 kap.). Confidential - only the tenant-owner may see their own entry. Never blended with the member register. Its extract also carries what the cooperative housing register is reported from: each transfer with its membership decision date, and any termination (upphörande) with the ground and the day it took effect - an entry listing holders and transfers with nothing saying the right itself ended would read as though the apartment were still held. |
| lägenhetsnummer | apartment number | Lantmäteriet numbering (e.g. 1101). Always rendered in the mono grid. |
| medlem | member | A person who is a member of the association. |
| medlemsförteckning | **member register** | Statutory register of members. BRL 9 kap. 8 § requires it and 9 § fixes its contents and its availability to anyone who asks; EFL 5 kap. 6-7 §§ apply through BRL 9 kap. 9 b §. Must never be blended with the apartment register in UI or data model. |
| meny | **menu** | The navigation on the association's website: a top level and one level under it, built from the association's own pages, the pages the platform generates and addresses elsewhere. The menu is also the ordering of the site - its first page entry is the front page, so there is no separate home-page setting. A visitor is offered only the entries they could open, so a page kept for the members is not named to anyone without a session. |
| moderering | **moderation** | What the board may do to a comment somebody wrote: strike it through, and nothing more. A hidden comment stays where it is, attributed exactly as it was before - a hide withholds words and never attribution, and a person with skyddade personuppgifter (protected personal data) is named to nobody either way - its text is withheld from readers and stays readable to the board and to whoever wrote it, and the act is recorded in the granskningslogg (audit log) with the date and the person who decided it. There is no deletion and no way back: what somebody wrote is a record of what was said, and a board that could erase one silently would be worse than one that can only strike it through. It is the board's own site:manage rather than a capability of its own, because a comment thread under a notice is part of what the association publishes. Possible for as long as the comment is there and whether or not the news item above it is still published: publication decides who may read a thread and who may write into one, never who may act on a comment already on one. |
| motion | **motion** | A member's own proposal put to the general meeting (motion till stämman). EFL 6 kap. 15 § gives a member the right to have an item treated at a general meeting if they ask the board in writing in time for the item to be taken up in the notice, and BRL 9 kap. 14 § applies that chapter to a housing cooperative with six exceptions of which this is not one - so the right is a **member's** and not a resident's: a partner, an adult child or a tenant living in the house has none of it. The same paragraph withholds it from a member who has been excluded even though the membership has not yet ended; Open BRF records no exclusion, so it cannot subtract that case. Received, never approved: the board records that it has the motion, and whether the proposal is adopted is the meeting's decision and is minuted there. Recording receipt is a separate act from putting the item into the notice (kallelse), and Open BRF performs only the first: a request that reached the board in time carries the right to have the item dealt with at the meeting, which EFL 6 kap. 22 § makes something the notice has to state clearly, while one that arrived later is an item the board may still take up. The kallelse itself arrives with the meetings module. Closed with a date and a status rather than deleted, because the record that a member put something to the meeting is the member's while the association holds it at all. Service tier: erased two years after it closed, on its own clock rather than on the retention policy that governs a former resident, while the minutes of the meeting stay in the document archive as association records. |
| motionstid | **motion deadline** | The date the association's own bylaws (stadgar) set for a motion to reach the board before a general meeting. The bylaws' clause and never the platform's: EFL 6 kap. 15 § says the request is to be made in the manner and within the time the bylaws determine where the bylaws contain provisions about it, so Open BRF holds no default and an association whose bylaws are silent has no date of its own for this setting to hold. That is not a cooperative without any timing rule: the paragraph's first sentence conditions the right on the written request reaching the board in time for the item to be taken up in the notice, and it does so whether or not the bylaws add a date. That condition turns on a notice date, which a platform holding no meetings cannot evaluate, so it is stated and never enforced here. Recorded as a recurring month and day, because a bylaws clause is a standing rule rather than one year's date. It decides which meeting a motion can reach and not whether one is received: intake stays open past it and the board decides what it can still take up. |
| mäklarinfo | broker information | The public page brokers and buyers expect: fees, transfer and lien note fees, building facts, links to bylaws and annual reports. Generated from association facts - never from the statutory registers. The transactional broker extract (mäklarbild) is a paid module, not core. |
| nyhet / nyheter | **news item** / **news** | What the board writes to the people who live in the house and to whoever reads the association's website. A news item is for the members unless the board publishes it to anyone, and it is served under /nyheter. It is the association's own writing rather than register data: monolingual, stored as written, and never translated. |
| nyhetsutskick | **news mailing** | The email that goes to the members when a news item is published, in each recipient's own language. The email specifically, not the act of reaching them: the text message is a smsutskick, claimed separately. Claimed once per item and never sent again - correcting a published notice mails nobody - and who it went to is written down as it stood at the moment of publication. |
| ombud | **proxy holder** | The person a member appoints to exercise their right at a general meeting when they are not there themselves (EFL 6 kap. 4 §), under a written authority (fullmakt). Only the member's spouse or cohabitant or another member may be one, and nobody may represent more than one member, unless the bylaws (stadgar) say otherwise - BRL 9 kap. 14 §, which replaces the three members EFL 6 kap. 5 § allows an economic association generally, so one is the default in a housing cooperative and not three. A member that is a legal person may be represented by a non-member; Open BRF holds natural persons only, so that case does not arise. Which limb of the eligibility rule the board relied on is recorded on the appointment, because the register can decide whether somebody is a member and nothing in Open BRF records who is anybody's spouse or cohabitant. On the voting register (röstlängd) as a line of its own: a member arriving with a neighbour's fullmakt is there as a member and as an ombud, with two votes and one body.
| ordförande | chair | Board chair - a position of trust (brass in the design system). |
| pantsättning / pantnotering | **lien** / lien note | Recorded in the apartment register; confidential. |
| pantsättningsavgift | pledge fee | What the association charges for noting a lien. Permitted by BRL 7 kap. 14 § only where the bylaws provide for it; that section sets no ceiling on it. An association fact - the policy and who pays it - and never a per-apartment amount. A pending version of that section, in force on a day the government sets, drops the pledge fee from the fees an association may charge at all, leaving the grant, transfer and second-hand letting fees; it follows the lien moving to the state register, which the lienholder reports rather than the association. |
| pass | **slot** | One bookable period of a bookable resource in the time-slot mode: a laundry room open 07:00 to 21:00 in two-hour slots offers seven a day. Fixed-length and derived from the resource's slot length and opening hours rather than stored, so narrowing the hours changes which slots exist without touching any booking; the length is carried on the resource as `slotMinutes`. What a booking claims, and the unit the database's one-live-booking-per-resource-and-start-time rule is written against. A slot that has begun reads as passed rather than as free, because a calendar of the week a resident is looking at has days in it that have gone. |
| personnummer | personal identity number | Swedish national ID. Only where legally justified; never in public views. |
| publiceringssamtycke | **publication consent** | A person's recorded consent to appear on a page the association publishes. No personal data reaches a public page without one, and protected personal data never reaches one at all. An image upload therefore declares whether it shows identifiable persons: that declaration is what ties a photograph to this consent. One consent covers one scope - a photograph, a name on the website, or the published board roster - and agreeing to one is not agreeing to another. It is granted and withdrawn as dated facts: a withdrawal closes the consent with a date and never deletes the record that it stood, because the period it covered is what says a page published then was published lawfully. The picture scope is named for that scope in code - the confirmation `photoConsentConfirmed` and the refusal `photo-consent-required` - and never for the consent as a whole; what the board reads on the screen says publiceringssamtycke. |
| pärm | **binder** | What a document in the archive is filed into - Protokoll, Stadgar, Trivselregler, Årsredovisning. The board names its own rather than picking from a fixed list, so it is free text the form only suggests from. Carried on the document as `category`, the archive's word for what a binder holds; the term for the binder itself is "binder", never "folder". |
| registerutdrag | data subject access report | Per-person GDPR report. |
| rättsligt bevarandekrav | **legal hold** | A recorded reason for keeping one person's service-tier data past its purge date: a dispute, an insurance matter, a request from an authority. GDPR art. 17.3 e (a legal claim to establish or defend) and art. 17.3 b (a legal obligation to keep the data) are the grounds it rests on: they disapply the right to erasure rather than supplying a basis for processing, which the association still needs under art. 6. It suspends purging for that person alone rather than changing the association's retention policy, and it is released with a date rather than deleted, so the record explains why nothing was purged in that period. |
| röstlängd | **voting register** | The list drawn up at a general meeting of the members, ombud and biträden present, stating how many votes the members have where they do not all have the same number (EFL 6 kap. 27 §). Drawn up by whoever opened the meeting or by the chair the meeting elected, approved by the meeting, and standing until the meeting resolves to change it. Derived and never stored in Open BRF, exactly as a booking allowance is: who was a member on the meeting day comes from the member register (medlemsförteckning), which bostadsrättslagen requires and which is append-only, and which apartment a membership covers comes from the residencies - a stored vote count would go stale the moment somebody moved or a transfer completed, and it would go stale silently. Every member has one vote, so two apartments are one vote, and members holding one bostadsrätt jointly have one vote between them (BRL 9 kap. 14 §). Taken into or appended to the minutes (protokoll), which is why it carries no erasure date of its own on a data subject access report.
| samtycke | **consent** | A recorded agreement that is the legal basis for processing personal data (GDPR art. 6.1 a). The board's samtycke to a plugin's stated permissions and personal-data categories is what lets it run, and a republished version that widens either is refused until the board consents again. The per-person case of appearing on a published page is publiceringssamtycke, below. |
| sida | **page** | One page on the association's own website, at its own address directly under the domain root. Written by the board, published or held as a draft, and either public or readable by anyone signed in - a page nobody may read is answered exactly as one that does not exist. Its body is a list of blocks and never markup, so the renderer decides what a block becomes and no stored page can carry a script or make the reader's browser fetch anything from a third party - a picture names a stored file by its id and never a URL. A link the board writes is the one address on a page that may leave this origin, and it is a navigation the visitor chooses rather than something fetched while the page is read. Service tier: the cooperative's own writing about itself, not register content. |
| skyddade personuppgifter | **protected personal data** | Persons with protected identity: masked in all public views, exports, and lists; access is logged. |
| sluten omröstning | **closed ballot** | A vote at a general meeting taken without recording who cast which vote, which is lawful on request and is the ordinary way an election is held. It is why a vote record in Open BRF carries no voter from the start: adding that column later would mean migrating a table the association's minutes are built from. Whether a vote was closed is recorded on the decision, because the minutes state how the vote fell (EFL 6 kap. 39 §). Nothing in the core casts a vote yet - the chair records each item's outcome as counts - and real-time voting is separate work.
| smsadapter | **SMS adapter** | The interface an SMS provider is written against, and the only way out of the platform by text message. One interface and a driver behind it, like the file storage: no provider's package is a dependency of the application, so a cooperative changes provider by changing a setting rather than by waiting for a release. An instance with no driver configured has no provider, which is where every instance starts. |
| smsutskick | **SMS mailing** | The text message that goes to the members when a news item is published: a headline and the address of the article, in each recipient's own language. Not a nyhetsutskick, which is the email - the two are asked for separately, claimed separately and reported separately, and a member reached both ways has been reached once each way. It goes to the members the association holds a number for; a member without one is not among the people it can reach this way, which is an absence rather than a failed delivery. Claimed once per item and never sent again. |
| stadgar | bylaws | The association's statutes. |
| styrelse | **board** | The elected board of the association. |
| styrelseledamot / ledamot | board member | |
| styrelselista | **board roster** | Who the association's board is, as the association publishes it: a name and an elected position, and nothing else. Each name requires that person's own publication consent (publiceringssamtycke) for this scope, which is not the same as consenting to a photograph, and a person carrying protected personal data (skyddade personuppgifter) is never published whatever they have consented to. Read from the register when the page is rendered, so a consent withdrawn today is off the page today. |
| suppleant | deputy board member | |
| systemroll | **system role** | A grant held against the instance rather than against an apartment or an election: administrator, and the external property manager. Separate from a position of trust, which is elected and dated, and from a residency, which follows the apartment. Held or not held rather than kept as history, and conferred from the person's own panel by an administrator. |
| taxeringsenhetsnummer | tax assessment unit number | The number identifying one taxeringsenhet - what is to be assessed on its own for property tax, which is the property itself unless something else is prescribed (fastighetstaxeringslagen (1979:1152) 4 kap. 1 §). Reported together with fastighetsbeteckning and fastighetstyp under Förordning (2026:898) 2 kap. 4 § andra stycket, in the same conditional case, and that förordning names the field without defining it. Not recorded, for the reason the property type gives. |
| tema | **theme** | An installable package that restyles an instance. The other package type beside a plugin, and not a kind of one: a theme is pure data - a manifest, light and dark token sets, fonts, a logo and a choice of core view variants - and contains no JavaScript, so it declares no permissions, handles no personal data, and puts nothing in the running process. It decides what a token looks like and never what it means, because colour carries legal semantics in a statutory register. Checked against a contrast matrix at install time, and installed or switched without a restart, since there is no code to load. |
| tillfälle | **occurrence** | One date in an event: the cleaning day on the 18th of April, not "cleaning days". A row of its own rather than a date computed from the event's recurrence, because a sign-up (anmälan) points at the date it is for and the places are counted per date. It carries the start and the end as instants on the association's own clock, so an event stated for ten in the morning is at ten in the morning on the two Sundays a year that are 23 and 25 hours long. Called off with a date rather than deleted, and one occurrence is called off without touching the rest of its event. |
| tillägg | **plugin** | An installable package that adds behaviour or a view to an instance. Installed from the curated catalog, declares what it may do and which personal data it handles, and is consented to by the board before it runs. Its code runs in the instance's own process, so installing or switching one on ends by restarting it. A theme is the other package type and is not a plugin. |
| tomträtt | site leasehold | The association holding its land on a leasehold from a public owner - the state, a municipality or other land in public ownership - rather than owning it. Governed by Jordabalken 13 kap. Recorded as an association fact, with the ground rent and the renegotiation date, because it is the first thing a broker asks about the land. |
| uppgiftsskyldighet | **supply duty** | The one-off duty to give Lantmäteriet the data the cooperative housing register is to hold, as distinct from the standing duty to report each later event (anmälningsskyldighet). Lag (2026:485) 3 § makes an association supply, by 31 December 2027, what the register is to hold about the bostadsrättslägenheten, the bostadsrättsföreningen, the bostadsrättshavaren, pantsättningar and anteckningar; 7 § lets Lantmäteriet order a defaulting association to do it in under penalty of a fine, and 6 § drops the duty for data obtainable from fastighetsregistret or lägenhetsregistret instead. **Pantsättningar are inside this duty although they open no anmälningsskyldighet for the association**: that standing per-event duty is the lienholder's (Lag (2026:484) 3 kap. 5 §), while the initial supply is the association's and covers the pantsättningar the register is to hold, which for an association means the pantnoteringar its own lägenhetsförteckning records - a prior note in the new register is no precondition, and the supply is owed for a lien the association holds whether or not one exists. 13 § of the introducing act is narrower than it first looks: it reaches a panträtt that was granted and given sakrättsligt skydd before Lag (2026:484) took effect **and was not noted under 11 §**, and such a lien keeps that skydd only if the information reached Lantmäteriet **within six months of that act taking effect**. A lien that was supplied is registered as a noterad pant under 11 § and is not at risk. Förordning (2026:898) 2 kap. 3-7 §§ is the field list and that förordning's övergångsbestämmelse 2 narrows this duty to a named subset of it, limited to what the association is obliged to have noted or has access to. Open BRF discharges it by producing an **initial supply** (`InitialSupply`): a documented file and a printable extract of the same rows, behind a capability of its own because it is the only file the platform writes a personnummer into. The file's shape is Open BRF's own, because the föreskrifter that would fix a real one are not issued: 2 kap. 2 § lets an anmälan be transferred electronically to Lantmäteriet under föreskrifter Lantmäteriet may issue, and 5 kap. 1-2 §§ leave the detail of registration and of the anmälan itself to that same authority. docs/register-supply-contract.md states each column, its statutory field and every listed field an instance does not hold. |
| upphörande | **termination** | A bostadsrätt ceasing to exist. A register event in its own right, distinct from a transfer to a new holder: reported by the cooperative within two weeks of the day it ceased (Lag (2026:484) 3 kap. 4 §), with Lantmäteriet able to order a late report in under penalty of a fine (3 kap. 10 §). Bostadsrättslagen as amended by Lag (2026:486) distinguishes two grounds and no more, and the model carries exactly those two: a föreningsstämma resolving that a bostadsrätt which has passed to the association shall cease rather than be transferred on (BRL 6 kap. 11 §), and the building the apartment is in being transferred or sold executively (BRL 7 kap. 33 §). Those two sections are also the only places BRL says a termination "ska registreras i bostadsrättsregistret". The alternatives inside the second - a voluntary disposal and an executive sale - are one ground here, because the statute states them in one sentence with one consequence and one reporting duty; which of the two happened is named in the board's reference. Statutory tier and strictly append-only, more strictly than a transfer or a lien note: those keep UPDATE because a lien is released and a mis-keyed entry corrected, while a bostadsrätt that has ceased has no later state to reach. Records the apartment, never a person - BRL 7 kap. 33 § ends every bostadsrätt in a disposed building at once, whoever holds them - and reaches a data subject access report through the member register instead. |
| upplåtelse | grant | The act of first granting a bostadsrätt to a holder; reported by the cooperative (Lag (2026:484), 3 kap.). The contract that does so is the upplåtelseavtal (grant agreement). |
| upplåtelseavtal | grant agreement | The contract first granting a bostadsrätt. |
| utdrag ur förteckning | **register extract** | A copy of a statutory register, produced on request. The member register extract is public; the apartment register extract goes to the board and to the tenant-owner it concerns. Distinct from the GDPR registerutdrag, which is a data subject access report. |
| utflyttad | moved out | Register state with dashed outline + purge date in the UI. |
| utskickskanal | **delivery channel** | Which way one recipient's copy of a mailing was sent: email or SMS. Carried on each row of the delivery ledger, so the two channels share one record of who the board addressed while each keeps its own claim, its own worker and its own report. |
| vanliga frågor | **FAQ** | The questions a housing cooperative is asked and the board's answers, carried in the block on the page rather than in a store of their own: it is the association's own writing about its own house, and the page it sits on is the thing that is published. Monolingual and stored as written, like every other piece of site content. An answer is one paragraph; an answer that needs several is a page, linked from here. |
| vistelse | **stay** | What a booking of a bookable resource in the date-range mode is to whoever makes it: a check-in, the nights between, and a check-out. Not a model of its own - a stay is one booking against a resource in the date-range mode, and its end is the local midnight after the last night, which is exactly the check-out date - so the word belongs to the interface and to the form that collects the two dates, and never to a table, a column or a payload field. Unbroken by definition: a night somebody else holds ends a stay rather than being passed over, which is why a range covering one is refused whole. |
| årsavgift / månadsavgift | annual fee / monthly fee | The fee the tenant-owner pays the association. |
| återkommande evenemang | **recurring event** | An event whose occurrences (tillfälle) repeat: every so many weeks on the same weekday, months on the same day of the month, or years on the same date. The rule states when it stops, and states exactly one end - a number of times or a last date - because the occurrences are written out when the event is saved and nothing extends them afterwards. It reaches at most two years past the first date; a rule reaching further is refused rather than quietly cut short. A monthly rule on the 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month and returns to the 31st afterwards, and an annual one on the 29th of February falls on the 28th in a common year. |
| återupptaget tillfälle | **reinstatement** | Putting a called-off occurrence (inställt tillfälle) back, so the date goes ahead after all. Its own act with its own entry in the granskningslogg (audit log) rather than the call-off undone: withdrawing an announcement and making one again are two decisions, and the row afterwards says only what the date is now. It brings no place back with it. Somebody who stood down (avanmälan) while the date was off has stood down, their withdrawal keeps the date it was given, and the place they gave up is free for anybody including them - at the back of the queue, and a board that wants them there asks them. Refused for a date that was never called off, and for one the clock has passed: it did not happen, and the calendar cannot say afterwards that it did. |
| ärende | **issue** | One reported problem with the building and the record of handling it, from new through in progress to done. The felanmälan (issue report) is how it arrives; the ärende is what the board and the property manager work. Service tier, and among the most sensitive free text the platform holds - a report about a leak or a neighbour carries health data and a third party's details without anybody intending it - so it is readable by its reporter and by whoever handles issues, and never published. |
| ärendetyp | **issue type** | The category an issue is reported under. Configured by the board, monolingual, and scoped to exactly one audience: non-member, member, or board (internal). The audience decides who is offered the type, and the filter is applied on the server for every caller - a resident is never shown the internal categories, and the form on the public website offers the non-member ones alone. |
| överlåtelse | transfer | Sale/transfer of a bostadsrätt between holders. |
| överlåtelseavgift | transfer fee | What the association charges when a bostadsrätt changes hands. Permitted by BRL 7 kap. 14 § only where the bylaws provide for it; that section sets no ceiling on it. An association fact - the policy and who pays it - and never a per-transfer amount. |
| överlåtelseavtal | **transfer agreement** | The contract transferring a bostadsrätt, as the upplåtelseavtal (grant agreement) is the one that first grants it. |
| överlåtelseavtalets referens | **agreement reference** | What the apartment register records to identify a transfer's transfer agreement: the board's own reference - a case number, or where the paper copy is filed - or the path of an uploaded copy. Every transfer states one, because the register extract lists it and a transfer cannot be removed once written. Never "contract number": the reference need not be a number and need not come from the contract. |

## Adding terms

Add the term in the same PR that introduces it in code, keep the table alphabetized by the Swedish column, and cite the statute at chapter level (only cite an exact section when verified against the current text at [riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se)).

Alphabetized in the Swedish alphabet, where å, ä and ö are letters in their own right and sort after z rather than as variants of a and o. `årsavgift` therefore follows `utflyttad`, and the `överlåtelse` rows come last.
